CreatorPulse Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Information We Access

CreatorPulse may request access to YouTube account and analytics information when a user chooses to connect a YouTube channel.

How We Use Information

We use authorized YouTube information to provide channel analytics, trend analysis, content opportunities and creator recommendations inside CreatorPulse.

Google and YouTube Data

CreatorPulse only accesses Google and YouTube data that the user explicitly authorizes. We do not sell Google or YouTube user data.

Data Storage

Authentication information and channel information may be stored as necessary to maintain the user's connection and provide the requested features.

Data Deletion

Users may disconnect their YouTube channel and request deletion of associated CreatorPulse data by contacting the developer.

Third-Party Services

CreatorPulse uses Google APIs and YouTube APIs to provide connected-channel functionality.
